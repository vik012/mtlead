Built for trust

What happens on the call and how it is handled.

  • What happens on a call: The receptionist greets the caller, asks for their intent, and captures the basics needed for follow-up.
  • What data is collected: Name, phone, email, loan type, urgency, and any notes the caller shares.
  • Where it goes: The summary is sent to your team and can be routed into your CRM or inbox workflow.
  • How it is secured: Calls and summaries are handled with access controls, secure storage, and privacy-first workflows.
